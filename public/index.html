BOOTSTRAP · PUBLIC PROJECTION

Richard Feynman Corpus

A public, versioned and source-verifiable projection of research on the works, lectures, reports, archival materials and scientific relations of Richard P. Feynman (1918–1988).

Core rule 
SOURCE_RECORD != CANONICAL_WORK
ANALOGY != IDENTITY
SIMILARITY != HISTORICAL_LINEAGE
UNKNOWN = VALID_RESULT

What will be published here

  • verified bibliography and chronology;
  • source and provenance index;
  • work/version lineage;
  • scientific-domain mappings;
  • public CSV/JSON exports;
  • explicit uncertainty and conflict states.

Research boundary

The historical Feynman corpus is reconstructed independently from the separate New Feyman Graph / New Feynman Graph research line. Structural resemblance is not treated as scientific validation.
